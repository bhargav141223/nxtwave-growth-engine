# 3-Minute Video Script

**[0:00 - 0:30] Introduce the problem and target student.**
"Hi, I'm Bhargav. Our challenge is to get 500 final-year engineering students to register for an AI workshop with just ₹2,000 in 7 days.
Final year students are stressed. They want to improve their resumes for placements, but they don't have time for a 10-week AI course. Our value proposition solves this: 'Build your first AI project in just 60 minutes.' It's fast, practical, and gives them a talking point for interviews."

**[0:30 - 1:00] Explain the 500-registration strategy.**
"A ₹2,000 budget is too small for standard Facebook ads. So, we're relying on a viral loop. 
Our strategy uses 3 channels:
First, organic reach in existing campus WhatsApp groups.
Second, spending ₹1,000 to sponsor two niche engineering meme pages for targeted top-of-funnel traffic.
Third, spending our final ₹1,000 on a referral leaderboard prize pool to incentivize students to invite their friends, dropping our Cost Per Acquisition to just 4 rupees."

**[1:00 - 2:00] Live demo of the working asset.**
*(Share screen of the Growth Engine prototype in Demo Mode)*
"Instead of a boring landing page, we built an interactive Growth Engine. 
When a student lands here, we don't just ask them to register. We ask two lightweight questions: 'What's your goal?' and 'What's your experience?' 
Based on their answers, the engine generates a personalized pitch. If they selected 'placements', it tells them exactly how this project helps their resume.
They register, and immediately hit our Referral Engine. Here, they get a unique link and see the live leaderboard. Because we tied our budget to the top 2 spots on this board, they are highly motivated to share the link in their class groups.
Behind the scenes, we have a Growth Dashboard tracking our North Star metric—500 registrations—along with CAC and channel distribution."

**[2:00 - 2:30] Explain the three growth experiments.**
"To optimize this funnel, we designed three A/B tests:
1. **Message Test**: Testing generic AI learning vs. placement-focused messaging to see which converts higher.
2. **Referral Test**: Testing a basic share button vs. our gamified leaderboard to measure the increase in average referrals per user.
3. **Personalization Test**: Testing a standard landing page vs. our quiz flow to ensure the personalized pitch offsets the friction of answering questions."

**[2:30 - 3:00] Explain how AI helped and what we rejected.**
"I used AI to brainstorm value propositions and write the boilerplate React code for this prototype. However, you have to use AI with good product judgment. 
For example, AI suggested blowing our entire ₹2,000 budget on Facebook Ads and focusing the messaging on 'Guaranteed Jobs.' I deliberately rejected both. Facebook ads need a larger budget to exit the learning phase, and final-year students are highly skeptical of fake job guarantees. I overruled the AI and focused on a grassroots community and referral strategy based on realistic student psychology. 
Thank you."
