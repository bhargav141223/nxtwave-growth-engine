# NxtWave AI Workshop - Growth Plan (5 Slides)

## SLIDE 1 — STUDENT + PROBLEM
**Target Audience**: Final-year engineering students (CS/IT/Electronics).
**Pain Point**: Placements are approaching, and their resumes lack modern, high-demand skills like AI. They are overwhelmed by long courses and need a quick win.
**Motivation**: They want to stand out in interviews without committing weeks of study time.
**Value Proposition**: "Build your first AI project in 60 minutes."
**Why they will register**: It promises a tangible, resume-ready outcome in a highly compressed timeframe with zero intimidation.

---

## SLIDE 2 — HOW WE GET 500 REGISTRATIONS
**Budget**: ₹2,000 | **Timeline**: 7 Days | **Target**: 500 Registrations

**Prioritized Channels & Assumptions**:
1. **WhatsApp Campus Communities (Organic)**
   - Reach: 5,000 | Conversion: 5% | Registrations: 250 | Cost: ₹0
2. **Viral Referral Loop (Incentivized)**
   - 20% of initial regs refer 3 friends | Registrations: 150 | Cost: ₹1,000 (Prizes)
3. **Niche Engineering Meme Pages (Paid Micro-Sponsorship)**
   - Reach: 10,000 | Conversion: 1% | Registrations: 100 | Cost: ₹1,000

**Funnel Model**:
Total Reach (15k+) → Landing Page (10% CTR = 1,500) → Registration (33% Conv. = 500)
**Estimated CAC**: ₹4.00 per registration.

---

## SLIDE 3 — WHAT WE BUILT (THE GROWTH ENGINE)
*We built a custom web application optimized for conversion and experimentation.*

**Visual Flow**:
1. **Discovery**: Micro-influencer shoutouts & peer WhatsApp links.
2. **AI Personalization**: 2-click interactive quiz (Goal + Experience).
3. **Dynamic Pitch**: Value proposition adapts based on answers.
4. **Registration**: Low-friction signup form.
5. **Referral Dashboard**: Gamified leaderboard with unique sharing links.
6. **Analytics Dashboard**: Tracks real-time CAC, funnel conversion, and referral rate.

## SLIDE 4 — THREE GROWTH TESTS

### 1. Message Test
**Hypothesis**: A placement-oriented message converts better than generic learning messaging.
**Test**: (A) "Build Your First AI Project" vs. (B) "Build an AI Project You Can Add to Your Resume".
**Metric**: Registration Conversion Rate.

### 2. Referral Test
**Hypothesis**: Visible progress and gamification increase sharing.
**Test**: (A) Basic share link vs. (B) Personal link + Competitive Leaderboard (Top 2 win ₹500).
**Metric**: Average successful referrals per registered student.

### 3. AI Personalization Test
**Hypothesis**: Tailoring the pitch to the student's goal increases desire to register.
**Test**: (A) Generic workshop pitch vs. (B) 2-question quiz leading to a customized pitch.
**Metric**: Registration Conversion Rate (must offset the friction of the quiz).

---

## SLIDE 5 — LEARNING + AI JUDGMENT

**What changed from first idea to final solution:**
We initially planned a standard landing page. We shifted to a "quiz-first" personalization engine because standard landing pages suffer from high bounce rates on mobile.

**AI Suggestion Rejected:**
AI suggested running Meta Ads for the entire ₹2,000 budget. We rejected this because algorithms require larger budgets to exit the "learning phase." Instead, we deployed the budget into high-leverage micro-influencers and a referral prize pool to force a viral loop within closed campus networks.

**If we had 24 more hours:**
We would implement an automated WhatsApp API integration to send the referral links directly to their phones post-registration, reducing drop-off in the sharing step.
